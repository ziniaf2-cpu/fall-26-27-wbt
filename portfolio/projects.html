Project 2: Computer Graphics

SMART CITY SIMULATION

The animation shows a realistic construction site with a changing day-to-night sky, moving clouds, and active construction machinery. The excavator and crane perform continuous movements, warning lights blink at night, building windows illuminate, and vehicles move along the road with headlights turned on.

Tech Stack: C++, OpenGL, FreeGLUT, Code::Blocks

Key Features:

  • Day and night transitions
  • Horizontal Cloud Drif
  • Excavator & Crane Movement
  • Blinking Warning Lights
  • Moving Vehicles & Headlights
  • Interactive keyboard controls
  • 2D transformations and timer-based animation
